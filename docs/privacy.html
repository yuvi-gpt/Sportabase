Sportabase Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Sportabase is a Chrome extension that provides AI-assisted analysis of sports articles and supported YouTube sports videos. It can generate summaries, classify content, evaluate evidence and reasoning, identify potentially speculative or exaggerated presentation, and provide multilingual analysis.

Sportabase runs only after the user intentionally opens or activates the extension. It does not continuously monitor browsing activity in the background.

Information Sportabase Processes

When a user requests an analysis, Sportabase may process the following information:

  • The active page URL and page title.
  • Visible article text extracted from the active page.
  • The available transcript and title of a supported YouTube video.
  • Information derived from the content, including summaries, classifications, scores, explanations, language information, and generated analysis.
  • Local interface preferences such as appearance, text size, density, overlay size, and overlay position.
  • A randomly generated installation identifier used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Technical and operational information such as request time, analysis type, model name, request status, token counts, cache status, and error information.
  • Network information such as an IP address that may be processed by the Sportabase backend or hosting provider when a request is made. An IP address may reveal an approximate geographic area.

How Sportabase Uses Information

Information is used only to provide, secure, maintain, and improve Sportabase's disclosed sports-analysis functionality. This includes:

  • Generating article and video summaries.
  • Identifying sports-content types, including match reports, transfer coverage, injuries, interviews, analysis, opinion, rumors, and official news.
  • Evaluating evidence, sourcing, reasoning, speculation, and presentation style.
  • Returning analysis and interface labels in the detected language of the source.
  • Preventing excessive or abusive use through global and per-installation usage limits.
  • Caching repeated analyses to reduce processing time and unnecessary AI requests.
  • Diagnosing errors and maintaining service reliability and security.

Local Browser Storage

Sportabase uses Chrome's local extension storage to save interface preferences and a randomly generated installation identifier.

The identifier is not a username, email address, or advertising identifier. It is used for operational rate limiting and abuse prevention.

Sportabase does not intentionally store raw article text or video transcripts in Chrome's local extension storage.

Backend Processing and Caching

Analysis requests are transmitted over HTTPS to the Sportabase backend hosted using Render.

The backend may temporarily cache generated analysis so that unchanged content can be returned without making another AI request.

  • Standard article and video analysis becomes ineligible for cache reuse after approximately six hours.
  • Live-commentary or live-update content becomes ineligible for cache reuse after approximately three minutes.
  • Cache records may contain the page URL, a cryptographic content hash, the generated response, content classification, timestamps, and operational metadata.
  • Raw article text and raw video transcripts are not intentionally stored inside the Sportabase analysis cache.

Expired records may remain until routine database cleanup occurs, but they are not treated as valid cached results.

Operational usage records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for rate limiting, security, debugging, service reliability, and compliance.

Third-Party Service Providers

Render

Render hosts the Sportabase backend. Render may process network, service, and technical information necessary to host and operate the backend, including request and service logs.

Render's privacy policy is available at: https://render.com/privacy

Google Gemini API

Sportabase uses the Google Gemini API to produce portions of its summaries, classifications, translations, and video analysis.

Depending on the requested feature, information sent to Gemini may include the page or video title, URL, extracted article text, video transcript, existing scoring explanations, detected language, and instructions required to generate the analysis.

Google's handling of submitted content depends on the Gemini API service and billing configuration. Google's terms state that content submitted through unpaid Gemini services, together with generated responses, may be used to provide, improve, and develop Google products and machine-learning technologies. Google also states that authorized human reviewers may process input and output from unpaid services.

Google states that prompts, contextual information, and generated outputs may be retained for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring, safety, legal, and regulatory purposes.

Users should not use Sportabase to analyze pages or transcripts containing sensitive, confidential, or private personal information.

Google's Gemini API terms and data policies are available at:

  • Gemini API Additional Terms
  • Gemini API Abuse Monitoring Policy

Information Sharing

Sportabase transfers information only when necessary to provide and protect its disclosed functionality, including transfers to the hosting and AI providers identified above.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal process.
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Protect users, Sportabase, or the public.

What Sportabase Does Not Do

  • Sportabase does not sell user data.
  • Sportabase does not use user data for targeted or personalized advertising.
  • Sportabase does not transfer user data to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • Sportabase does not use browsing information to determine creditworthiness, insurance eligibility, employment eligibility, or lending decisions.
  • Sportabase does not intentionally collect passwords, authentication cookies, payment-card information, private messages, or health records.
  • Sportabase does not analyze pages unless the user intentionally activates the extension.
  • Sportabase does not bypass paywalls or access controls.

Security

Sportabase uses HTTPS when communicating with its backend. Access to backend services and AI credentials is restricted to the server environment.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Sportabase uses reasonable technical and operational safeguards, but users should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential content.

User Choices and Control

  • Users decide when Sportabase performs an analysis.
  • Users may close the Sportabase overlay without performing an analysis.
  • Users may remove the extension to stop future collection and remove its locally stored extension data.
  • Users may contact Sportabase to ask questions or request deletion of identifiable backend data, where technically feasible and legally required.

Age Requirement

Sportabase is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. It is not directed toward children or individuals under 18.

Automated Analysis Disclaimer

Sportabase uses automated rules and AI-assisted systems. Its summaries, classifications, scores, translations, and verdicts may be incomplete or incorrect.

A high score does not guarantee that a claim is true, and a low score does not prove that a claim is false. Users should review the original source and use their own judgment.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Sportabase's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Sportabase's functionality, service providers, or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
