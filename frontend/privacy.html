Privacy and your data

Sportabase account product lab · September 2026

Clerk handles sign-in and session security. Sportabase stores an internal account identity, installation IDs, preferences, watches and alerts. Installation IDs are random; we do not fingerprint devices.

My Activity stores analysis titles, timestamps and revisit links. It does not copy article bodies or transcripts. You can stop saving activity, clear it, or export personal data in Settings.

Optional product analytics stores narrow event names, platform and date for up to 90 days. It never records article bodies, transcripts, authentication tokens, push credentials, free-form event payloads, IP addresses, user agents, referrers or arbitrary URLs. Disabling usage sharing removes retained optional account usage events.

Necessary operational records are separate from optional analytics. While an account is active, Sportabase retains its lifecycle state, registered installations, watch ownership and notification registration state so those features can operate securely. Disabling optional analytics does not remove those records.

Push credentials are used only to deliver notifications. Turning off push does not remove in-app Alerts. Account deletion removes account-private data while preserving canonical public sports intelligence. The deletion tombstone retains an opaque Sportabase row ID, a deterministic domain-separated hash of the Clerk subject, and deleted status solely to stop an old session from recreating the account. Settings, revisions, lifecycle timestamps and first-analysis state are erased or zeroed.

This product lab requires configured authentication and a completed live security review before release. This policy describes the lab's implemented data controls.
